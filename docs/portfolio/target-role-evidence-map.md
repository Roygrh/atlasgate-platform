## Foundation evidence

The current repository also demonstrates documentation-only foundation practices: English tracked artifacts, private-context isolation, reconstruction guidance based on observable state, stable discovery identifiers, and traceable separation among stakeholder speech, preliminary analysis, obligation candidates, control objectives, requirements, controls, and decisions. SES-001 demonstrates simulated executive discovery; SES-002 demonstrates product discovery; SES-003 demonstrates security/compliance facilitation, scenario-specific context, legal uncertainty, exception governance, audit-evidence definition, minimization, and evidence traceability. These artifacts are governance and facilitation evidence, not evidence of implemented platform behavior, certification, legal validation, penetration testing, observability, performance, or deployment.
