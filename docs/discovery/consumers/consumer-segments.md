# Preliminary Consumer Segments

These segments derive from the fictional simulated [SES-002 transcript](../sessions/SES-002-platform-product-discovery/01-simulated-transcript.md). None is validated or approved product scope.

## SEG-001 - Domain API producer

- **Primary jobs:** Translate a domain need into an API change or integration; coordinate review, testing, release, operation, and consumer communication.
- **Current alternatives:** Domain notes, direct messages, copied prior configuration, local trackers, and temporary access arrangements.
- **Needs or pain points:** Complete inputs, visible handoffs, appropriate review timing, known consumers, and domain autonomy without duplicate queues.
- **Evidence:** STMT-021, STMT-026, STMT-030, STMT-031.
- **Important differences:** Partner launches have commercial dates and external dependencies; internal changes depend on consumer release trains.
- **Validation needed:** Compare completed and delayed commerce and loyalty samples across more domains.
- **Owner:** Daniel Okafor
- **Status:** Under validation

## SEG-002 - Internal API consumer

- **Primary jobs:** Discover behavior, assess change impact, test, schedule adoption, and obtain semantic support.
- **Current alternatives:** Internal directories, team channels, direct producer contact, and cloned examples or configuration.
- **Needs or pain points:** Early notice, compatibility information, usable examples, known ownership, and release flexibility.
- **Evidence:** STMT-022, STMT-029, STMT-031.
- **Important differences:** Internal consumers may have organizational access but independent release cadences and incomplete dependency visibility.
- **Validation needed:** Interview consuming mobile and commerce teams and inspect two change records.
- **Owner:** Lucas Pereira
- **Status:** Preliminary

## SEG-003 - External partner developer

- **Primary jobs:** Discover eligible APIs, gain access, test an integration, enter production, receive change notice, and obtain support.
- **Current alternatives:** Business-contact introductions, emailed documents, partner-specific calls, and support routed through integration coordinators.
- **Needs or pain points:** Clear eligibility, current documentation, individual access, environment status, actionable support, and audience-appropriate information.
- **Evidence:** STMT-023, STMT-032, STMT-033, STMT-034.
- **Important differences:** Partner type, procurement, identity evidence, location, contract context, and integration frequency vary.
- **Validation needed:** Direct fictional partner interviews plus sampled onboarding and support records.
- **Owner:** Sofia Novak
- **Status:** Preliminary

## SEG-004 - Security or compliance reviewer

- **Primary jobs:** Assess applicable obligations and evidence, review risk, and clarify routine approval and exception routes.
- **Current alternatives:** Existing review channels, returned requests, email coordination, and unverified exception packaging; no source record was inspected.
- **Needs or pain points:** Timely and complete flow context, authoritative obligation sources, accountable decisions, purpose-specific evidence, minimization, and expiry/closure visibility.
- **Evidence:** STMT-021, STMT-025, STMT-028; SES-001 STMT-007 through STMT-010 and STMT-016; SES-003 STMT-048, STMT-054 through STMT-056, STMT-060, STMT-062 through STMT-065, STMT-072, STMT-075.
- **Important differences:** Security advice, compliance interpretation, business accountability, and formal approval may differ by asset, flow, environment, and source.
- **Validation needed:** Sanitized review, access, exception, audit-evidence, and source-interpretation records with direct reviewer evidence.
- **Owner:** Elena García
- **Status:** Preliminary

## SEG-005 - Platform operator and support team

- **Primary jobs:** Operate shared foundations, route requests, support environments, diagnose ownership, and maintain sustainable shared service.
- **Current alternatives:** Tickets, chat escalation, manual lookups, returned forms, and copied configuration.
- **Needs or pain points:** Complete requests, visible domain and partner context, ownership sources, dependency visibility, and bounded support responsibility.
- **Evidence:** STMT-024, STMT-027, STMT-030, STMT-036.
- **Important differences:** Platform operation does not confer ownership of domain semantics or commercial commitments.
- **Validation needed:** Ticket samples, support routing, operational workshop, and ownership-source comparison.
- **Owner:** Jordan Lee
- **Status:** Under validation
