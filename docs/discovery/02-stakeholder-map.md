# Stakeholder Map

Northstar Digital Group and every person or group below are fictional. Names make the simulated discovery concrete; they do not represent real individuals or completed interviews.

| Fictional stakeholder | Role | Goals | Concerns | Decision influence | Expected discovery contribution | Likely conflicts or trade-offs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Maya Chen | Executive sponsor | Connect platform investment to business outcomes and accountable delivery | Cost, delivery risk, adoption, and unclear ownership | Approves sponsorship, funding direction, and outcome priorities | Strategic drivers, constraints, success measures, and escalation model | Speed and breadth versus evidence, focus, and sustainable adoption |
| Daniel Okafor | API platform product manager | Establish a useful platform proposition and adoption path | Building unused capabilities and weak measures | Shapes product priorities and proposed scope | Consumer segments, problem framing, roadmap inputs, and outcome hypotheses | Shared consistency versus domain-specific needs |
| Priya Raman | Platform architect | Create coherent boundaries and evolvable technical direction | Premature technology choices and accidental coupling | Recommends architecture decisions; does not alone approve business scope | Current landscape, quality concerns, constraints, and decision drivers | Long-term coherence versus near-term delivery |

Decision influence is provisional. The executive kickoff and subsequent workshops must clarify actual decision rights, veto conditions, and conflict-resolution paths.
