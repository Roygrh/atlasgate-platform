# SES-001 - Executive Kickoff: Session Brief

> **Fictional-simulation disclaimer:** Northstar Digital Group, this session, and all participants are fictional. This portfolio artifact is a planned simulation, not a record of a real-world meeting.

## Objectives and agenda

Objectives are to capture attributed motivations, distinguish outcome candidates from targets, expose tensions, agree discovery-only boundaries, clarify what remains undecided, and assign evidence collection. The 60-minute agenda is: framing and rules (5 minutes), business context (15), delivery and adoption (10), security and reliability (10), scope and decision rights (10), and follow-ups (10).

## Planned questions

- What problem appears important now, and whose evidence supports it?
- Which business or engineering outcomes might justify investment?
- What adoption, security, reliability, cost, ownership, and delivery-speed concerns compete?
- What is excluded from discovery and from this meeting?
- Who may prioritize, approve, veto, or grant exceptions, and where is authority unknown?
- Which inventories, journey samples, incident records, review records, and cost data should be collected next?

## Evidence-capture rules

Record speech with attribution and section anchors. Preserve estimates as unverified. Record challenges beside the claims they challenge. Do not create requirements, findings, or decisions inside the dialogue. Analysis may later cite statements, but one session cannot validate broad enterprise claims.

## Non-goals

The session will not approve requirements or scope, select technologies or vendors, settle architecture, define numeric targets, create diagrams, authorize implementation, or claim enterprise-wide validation.

## Exit criteria

All six participants contribute; at least three substantive tensions are visible; discovery-process decisions and non-decisions are separated; follow-ups have fictional owners; and the transcript can be traced into statements, preliminary findings, questions, risks, and outcome candidates without promoting any item to an approved requirement.
