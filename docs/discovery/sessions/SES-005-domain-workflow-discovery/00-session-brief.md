# SES-005 - Domain Workflow Discovery: Session Brief

> **Fictional simulation:** This session, organization, participants, workflows, systems, examples, estimates, and evidence are fictional portfolio material. Nothing here records a real meeting or validated enterprise fact.

## Purpose and participants

Compare Commerce and Loyalty API-delivery workflows deeply enough to separate shared concerns, necessary domain variation, possible accidental inconsistency, and unresolved evidence. Participants are Daniel Okafor (facilitator and API Platform Product Manager), Aisha Bello (Commerce Domain Lead), Lucas Pereira (Loyalty Domain Lead), Jordan Lee (Platform Engineering Representative), Priya Raman (Platform Architect), and Marcus Reed (SRE and Observability Lead).

## Objectives and agenda

1. Establish evidence rules and compare triggers, actors, meaning, review, progression, release, support, incidents, and completion.
2. Walk four distinct cases: Commerce partner launch, Commerce tenant-specific policy change, Loyalty semantic API change, and Loyalty consumer migration/version transition.
3. Ask which steps are shared; which differ through business context; which differences are accidental or necessary; where ownership transfers or only appears to; which handoffs lose time or information; which local practices must not become global standards; which common capabilities could support variation; and what evidence must precede requirements.
4. Assign evidence requests without approving a workflow, requirement, ownership model, architecture, mechanism, or technology.

## Preparation

Review SES-001 through SES-004 statements and findings, current journeys, segments, security evidence questions, reliability boundaries, outcomes, risks, assumptions, and open questions. Bring only fictional recollections; label estimates and distinguish observed claims from interpretation.

## Required scenarios

- A Commerce partner launch with an external date, partner coordination, Commerce-owned behavior, review dependencies, and possible production criticality.
- A Commerce tenant-specific policy change with urgent pressure and unresolved exception authority; no policy mechanism may be selected.
- A Loyalty semantic change affecting balance, eligibility, or reward interpretation for internal consumers with asynchronous adoption.
- A Loyalty consumer migration/version transition with multiple schedules, possible temporary coexistence as a problem, and unclear completion ownership.

## Evidence rules

Statements remain attributed. Simulation alone validates nothing. Estimates must be marked unverified. Coordination is not authority; producer and consumer responsibility remain separate; deployment and change completion remain separate. SES-003/SES-004 questions may be cross-linked but their unresolved controls, indicators, and targets are not reopened or decided.

## Non-goals

No universal or target workflow, requirement, RACI, domain boundary, policy mechanism, architecture, technology, vendor, contract, numeric target, implementation plan, or implementation artifact will be approved or selected.

## Exit criteria

All six perspectives and four scenarios are represented; conflicts, workarounds, ownership transitions, dependencies, adoption needs, and completion ambiguity remain visible; discovery-method decisions and explicit non-decisions are recorded; and evidence requests have fictional owners.
