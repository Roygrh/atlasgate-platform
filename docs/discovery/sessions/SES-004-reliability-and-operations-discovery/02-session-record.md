# SES-004 - Reliability and Operations Discovery Record

> **Fictional simulation:** This record describes a fictional portfolio workshop. Participants, incidents, telemetry, estimates, and evidence are simulated and do not describe a real employer or production service.

## Summary and participants

Daniel Okafor facilitated Marcus Reed, Jordan Lee, Aisha Bello, Lucas Pereira, Sofia Novak, and Priya Raman. The workshop produced preliminary critical-journey, incident, ownership/support, telemetry, and SLI candidates while refusing unsupported targets or commitments.

## Objective results

- Criticality was framed by consumer, outcome, consequence, and period rather than API count or a universal tier.
- Four scenarios remained distinct and exposed different domain, platform, consumer, partner, support, and coordination boundaries.
- Detection, diagnosis, mitigation, restoration, communication, closure, and durable evidence were separated.
- Infrastructure health was rejected as sufficient user-outcome evidence.
- Candidate indicators were recorded without numeric targets or claims of measurability.

## Scenarios

The record covers commerce launch degradation, loyalty behavior after change, cross-boundary ownership uncertainty, and production support constrained by telemetry/access gaps. No scenario is a real incident or has a final severity.

## Themes and tensions

Meaningful tensions included universal versus journey-specific reliability; infrastructure health versus consumer outcomes; domain autonomy versus cross-boundary coordination; reachable platform support versus actual accountability; rapid diagnosis versus safe access; operational telemetry versus durable evidence; local workarounds versus hidden demand; and signal coverage versus false positives and fatigue. Information remained incomplete and conflicting, including two explicitly unverified duration/contact estimates.

## Discovery-process decisions

Only method decisions were made: keep scenarios distinct; label estimates unverified; separate internal active time from external waiting; record lifecycle stages separately; and require source-backed validation before proposing targets.

## Explicit non-decisions

No SLI, SLO, SLA, error budget, numeric target, severity, ownership model, support commitment, requirement, architecture, telemetry design, access control, incident process, or product/tool was approved or selected.

No incident, change, telemetry, support, ownership, access, contract, traffic, recovery, or partner record was inspected. Estimates, prevalence, causal attribution, criticality, coverage, integrity, retention, authority, and measurability remain unverified.
