# SES-006 - External Consumer Discovery: Session Brief

This is a fictional, simulated interview for the AtlasGate professional portfolio. Rachel Kim, Meridian Retail Partners, Northstar Digital Group, and all other participants and accounts are fictional. No real meeting or source record is represented.

## Purpose and method

Obtain a direct, independent external-consumer account and compare it with internal SES-001 through SES-005 assumptions and preliminary SES-007 architecture questions. Walk through four contrasting cases, probe actual completion and waiting boundaries, ask what internal accounts got wrong, and capture conflicting views. The account is one simulated consumer perspective, not a representative sample or a contractual source.

## Participants

- Daniel Okafor - API Platform Product Manager and facilitator
- Sofia Novak - External Partner Integration Lead
- Elena García - Security Architect
- Aisha Bello - Commerce Domain Lead
- Jordan Lee - Platform Engineering Representative
- Rachel Kim - Senior Integration Engineer, Meridian Retail Partners; independent external consumer

## Scope and guardrails

Cover discovery, fit, onboarding, prerequisites, credential issuance and usable access, test access, documentation usability, examples and semantics, change notice and compatibility, independent consumer scheduling, traffic expectations, support, incident communication, safe evidence, ownership, launch readiness, and migration closure. Probe first-time onboarding, an access problem, a semantic or breaking change, and a traffic/support incident. Ask which internal claims are wrong or overstated and which SES-007 questions gain evidence. No requirement, mechanism, quota, version policy, service commitment, architecture, or implementation is approved.

## Evidence to request

Request sanitized onboarding timelines and returned requests, access failure and handoff records, documentation tasks and interpretation questions, change notices and consumer release decisions, traffic/support incident timeline and safely shareable diagnostics, and contract/authority sources. Assign owners in the closing exchange; treat recalled quantities as unverified.
