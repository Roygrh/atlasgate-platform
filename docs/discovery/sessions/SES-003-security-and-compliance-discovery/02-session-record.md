# SES-003 - Security and Compliance Discovery Record

> **Fictional simulated activity:** Northstar Digital Group, all participants, examples, records, estimates, policies, obligations, and incidents in this record are fictional and created for the AtlasGate professional portfolio.

## Summary and participants

On 2026-08-06, Daniel Okafor facilitated Elena García, Henry Walsh, Aisha Bello, Sofia Novak, Priya Raman, and Jordan Lee through 36 exchanges. The simulation explored assets, boundaries, identity and access lifecycle, review timing, obligations, threats, exceptions, evidence, minimization, retention uncertainty, and support access. It established preliminary context only.

## Objective results and scenarios

- **Commerce partner access:** distinguished customer/order/transaction-related assets, partner and environment boundaries, incomplete identity inputs, access lifecycle, and mixed delay attribution.
- **Loyalty internal change:** challenged the assumption that internal identity eliminates risk and exposed cloned-access, downstream-use, audience, and evidence-purpose questions.
- **Urgent launch exception:** separated deviation, authority, safeguards, monitoring, expiry, reassessment, removal, and closure; authority remains unresolved.
- **Operational support access:** exposed tension between rapid diagnosis and attributable, purpose- and time-bounded access across platform, domain, and partner boundaries.

## Themes and tensions

Security review is not established as the primary delay; queue, rework, missing inputs, active assessment, and external waiting need separate evidence. Participants rejected a universal checklist, a single identity or approval model, centralized enforcement as a default, Security-only business-risk acceptance, and full operational data retention as audit proof. Audit or decision evidence and operational telemetry have different purposes. Evidence minimization and privacy questions remain unresolved without legal conclusions.

## Discovery-process decisions

The session will preserve scenario-specific records, treat obligation applicability and retention as source-validation questions, distinguish control objectives from controls, and request sanitized or metadata-first evidence. These are discovery-process choices, not security requirements.

## Non-decisions

No asset classification, obligation, retention period, authority, requirement, control objective, control, product, technology, architecture, exception, or ADR was approved. No completed threat model or threat-model diagram was produced.

## Limitations

This was one fictional simulation. No source record, policy, contract, law, identity system, access record, exception, incident, telemetry source, or retention schedule was inspected. Estimates are explicitly unverified. Direct internal consumers, external developers, legal counsel, privacy specialists, operations leadership, and formal decision authorities were absent.
