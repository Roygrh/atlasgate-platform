# SES-003 - Security and Compliance Discovery Brief

> **Fictional simulated activity:** Northstar Digital Group, every participant, scenario, policy, record, estimate, incident, obligation, and evidence item in this brief are fictional and created only for the AtlasGate professional portfolio.

- **Date:** 2026-08-06
- **Facilitator:** Daniel Okafor, distinguishes statements from analysis and assigns evidence follow-ups.
- **Security perspective:** Elena García, frames assets, threats, control objectives, and review questions without selecting controls.
- **Compliance perspective:** Henry Walsh, distinguishes possible obligations and audit evidence from verified applicability.
- **Domain perspective:** Aisha Bello, contributes commerce journeys, urgency, and ownership tensions.

## Objectives and agenda

Explore protected assets; trust and responsibility boundaries; human, service, organization, and environment identities; access request through removal; review timing and delay components; possible obligations by flow; exception lifecycle; audit evidence; retention and minimization; support access; and threat-model inputs. The agenda is: evidence rules, assets and boundaries, four scenario walkthroughs, cross-scenario tensions, and owned evidence requests.

## Preparation and questions

Review SES-001 and SES-002 as unvalidated simulated evidence. Bring sanitized examples of review paths, access lifecycle records, exception records, policy or contractual source categories, support-access records, and evidence inventories if available. Ask what is protected, where responsibility changes, who or what acts, which source may create an obligation, what decision evidence is needed, what operational telemetry exists, when review occurs, why time is spent, who may authorize deviations, and how expiry and closure are demonstrated.

## Required scenarios

1. Commerce partner access involving customer, order, or transaction-related information.
2. Loyalty internal change involving customer identifiers, reward balances, or eligibility data.
3. A dated partner launch requesting an exception because review or access evidence is incomplete.
4. Operational support access across production domain and partner boundaries.

## Evidence and sensitive-information rules

Statements remain attributed claims; policy or legal interpretation remains separate from verified obligation; control objectives remain separate from selected controls; audit evidence remains separate from operational telemetry. Record uncertainty, conflicts, source needs, and owners. Do not copy credentials, tokens, personal data, customer records, sensitive configuration, full logs, or confidential policy text. Prefer metadata, redacted samples, aggregates, purpose limitation, and minimum necessary evidence. This exercise provides no legal advice or legal conclusion.

## Non-goals

Do not approve requirements, obligations, control objectives, exceptions, products, technologies, architecture, ADRs, retention periods, or implementation. Do not create a threat-model diagram or claim that a control or evidence source exists.

## Exit criteria

All seven perspectives and four scenarios are represented; disagreements and unverified estimates remain visible; assets, boundaries, obligations, threats, exceptions, and evidence needs are distinguishable; exception expiry and closure are addressed; evidence requests have fictional owners; and no legal applicability, requirement, control, architecture, or implementation is approved.
