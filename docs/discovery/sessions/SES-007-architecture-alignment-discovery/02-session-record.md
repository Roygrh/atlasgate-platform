## Tensions and rejected premature conclusions

The group retains platform consistency versus domain autonomy, centralized governance versus queue risk, modular monolith versus premature distribution, management availability versus runtime continuity, platform metadata versus domain meaning, traceability versus data and process cost, synchronous simplicity versus asynchronous resilience, centralized policy management versus enforcement locality, operational visibility versus ownership confusion, and partner needs versus missing external evidence. It explicitly rejects the claim that asynchronous Loyalty adoption requires distributed event architecture. It also declines to infer a universal approval route or control-plane structure from project direction.
