# SES-007 - Architecture Alignment Discovery: Session Brief

This is a fictional, simulated workshop for the AtlasGate portfolio, not a real meeting or architecture approval. Northstar Digital Group and all participants are fictional.

## Purpose and preparation

Daniel Okafor facilitates Priya Raman, Elena García, Marcus Reed, Jordan Lee, Aisha Bello, and Lucas Pereira. Use the preliminary [SES-001–SES-005 evidence](../README.md), [findings](../../findings/preliminary-findings.md), [risks](../../../project-management/risk-register.md), and [decision governance](../../../architecture/decisions/README.md). SES-006 external-consumer discovery is incomplete. Compare architecture drivers, responsibility and failure boundaries, alternatives, decision criteria, future ADR topics, and missing evidence.

## Discussion tests

- Test platform versus domain authority, governance versus execution, and whether runtime handling should depend on administrative workflows.
- Test policy definition, evaluation, and enforcement; domain semantics and platform metadata; publication, revision, change history, and configuration distribution as candidate concerns.
- Test independent failure handling, telemetry production versus interpretation, incident coordination versus risk acceptance, and synchronous versus asynchronous change propagation.
- Compare centralized, modular, and distributed control-plane directions without selecting one. Use Commerce launch/change, Loyalty semantic change/adoption, urgent tenant exception, and cross-boundary incident scenarios.
- Identify which choices need external-consumer evidence and which may merit future ADRs.

## Method and exit

Record meaningful disagreement and owner-assigned evidence requests. Separate dialogue from later interpretation. A workshop statement is not a verified source, driver validation, accepted boundary, approved requirement, ADR, or implemented architecture. No target design, technology, diagram, architecture baseline, or policy control will be approved here.
