This is fictional simulated portfolio evidence about fictional Northstar Digital Group and fictional stakeholders. It is not a real meeting or source audit.

- **Purpose:** Test whether SES-001 through SES-007, including the later recorded SES-006 consumer account, provide sufficient bounded evidence to close I01 Business Discovery and hand off to requirements and architecture analysis.

- **Participants:** Maya Chen, Daniel Okafor, Priya Raman, Elena García, Marcus Reed, Aisha Bello, Lucas Pereira, Jordan Lee, and Sofia Novak.

Rachel Kim's SES-006 testimony is represented through its cited record; she is not a participant.
- **Method:** Compare independent and single-perspective accounts; distinguish stakeholder report from interpretation and evidence sufficiency from factual certainty; retain conflicts and owner-assigned validation tasks. Evaluate all 20 review questions in the [transcript](01-simulated-transcript.md).
- **Exit test:** Determine whether the discovery problem frame, actors, contrasting journeys, recurring concerns, and open evidence work are clear enough to proceed. Record residual uncertainty and exact baseline artifacts. Closing discovery does not approve requirements, architecture, legal applicability, targets, technology, or implementation.
