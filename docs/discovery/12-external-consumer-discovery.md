# External Consumer Discovery

This is a preliminary synthesis of fictional, simulated [SES-006](sessions/SES-006-external-consumer-discovery/02-session-record.md). Rachel Kim and Meridian Retail Partners are fictional. One direct consumer account is useful contradictory evidence, not representative or contractual proof.

## Onboarding, access, documentation, and testing

The [consumer-need](external-consumers/consumer-needs.md) and [onboarding-dependency](external-consumers/onboarding-dependencies.md) records distinguish finding a listed API from confirming use-case fit; receiving a document from using its examples and domain semantics; submitting prerequisites from seeing accepted handoffs; and credential issuance from a successful authorized call in the intended test environment (STMT-176 through STMT-185). A first-time partner needs an interpretable support route and its own release acceptance before production readiness. Partner procurement and release pauses must be separated from provider-controlled waiting. Rachel prefers both self-service routine facts and human semantic clarification; neither is an approved service requirement.

## Change, compatibility, and completion

The [change analysis](external-consumers/change-and-compatibility.md) distinguishes syntax from business meaning, sent notice from comprehension, and producer deployment from consumer adoption (STMT-192 through STMT-199). Meridian has an independent release schedule in the simulation. Advance notice is a preference pending contract evidence. Compatibility, version labels, migration closure, residual old use, and acceptance authority need sampled changes and agreements. No versioning policy or notice interval is selected.

## Traffic, support, incident communication, and ownership

The [traffic analysis](external-consumers/traffic-expectations.md) asks for expected usage shape, planned-spike context, and interpretable symptoms before any traffic-control choice (STMT-200 through STMT-201). The [support analysis](external-consumers/support-and-communication.md) requires examination of accepted routing, verified impact communication, safe diagnostic context, and consumer-observed recovery (STMT-185 through STMT-206). Reachable Platform Support and Sofia can coordinate; Commerce owns its business meaning, Security has applicable access/disclosure authority, and risk acceptance remains unresolved. Transparent incident updates must be purpose-limited and sanitized. No quota, rate limit, retry behavior, SLA, SLO, support frequency, or service commitment is approved.

## Workarounds, confirmed concerns, and challenges

Borrowed test access accelerates one local task while shifting attribution, scope, cleanup, and support risk (STMT-188). The consumer account directly supports prior concerns about cross-boundary routing, semantic ownership, producer/consumer duties, and completion beyond deployment (FND-004, FND-029 through FND-032, FND-034). It challenges the sufficiency of centralized documentation, credential delivery, notice sent, and platform reachability as proxies for usable access, change understanding, or accountable ownership (STMT-178, STMT-184, STMT-186, STMT-199). It does not establish prevalence or invalidate shared-context hypotheses.

## Architecture implications and non-decisions

SES-006 supports continued analysis of [driver DRV-006](architecture/architecture-drivers.md), platform/domain metadata authority, publication versus adoption, and signal production versus consumer outcome interpretation. It challenges any alternative evaluated only on administrative simplicity or a single provider completion event. It leaves management/runtime failure behavior, modular versus distributed style, enforcement placement, revision mechanism, and architecture approval unresolved. [SES-007](11-architecture-alignment-discovery.md) remains preliminary discovery; decisions belong to a future Architecture Baseline assessment. No architecture, ADR, diagram, requirement, authentication mechanism, compatibility policy, technology, target, or implementation is approved.
