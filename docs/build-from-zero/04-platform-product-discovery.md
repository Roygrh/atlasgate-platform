## Repeatable method

1. Verify the branch, clean worktree, remote alignment, guidance, and private-plan isolation.
2. Prepare a fictional brief with bounded objectives and non-goals.
3. Simulate distinct journeys with all required perspectives, tensions, conflicting information, estimates, workarounds, adoption barriers, candidate measures, and owner-assigned evidence requests.
4. Extract sequential attributed statements with transcript anchors.
5. Derive segments, journeys, hypotheses, and findings while preserving limitations and rejection conditions.
6. Propagate evidence into existing registers and indexes without approving scope, requirements, measures, architecture, or implementation.
7. Validate links, identifiers, statuses, participant coverage, prohibited scope, Git state, and private-plan isolation.

Confirm all `STMT`, `FND`, `SEG`, `JRN`, and `HYP` references exist and are unique; every segment and journey cites evidence; every hypothesis has a rejection signal; all six transcript participants appear in the stakeholder map; no finding, segment, or journey is `Validated`; no hypothesis is `Supported`; later sessions remain planned; no requirement or architecture is approved; and no implementation artifact exists. ## Limitations

This iteration contains fictional recalled accounts, not reviewed operational records or direct partner interviews. It establishes preliminary traceability and a repeatable discovery method only. It does not establish baselines, targets, product scope, roadmap priority, cost-benefit, architecture, technology, or executable behavior.
